Choosing a drop-in daycare

A practical checklist for parents comparing drop-in child care options in Jacksonville, NC.

Drop-in child care gives you the flexibility of a babysitter with the reliability of a child care center. It's ideal for errands, appointments and last-minute plans. But not every drop-in option is the same. Here's what to look for before you leave your child anywhere.

A drop-in checklist

  • Safety first. Ask whether staff are CPR and first aid certified, how the entrance is controlled and whether the facility has security cameras.
  • Attention. Smaller centers can offer more one-on-one attention, so your child isn't just another face in the crowd.
  • Clear hours and limits. Know the opening hours, the ages served and how long children can stay.
  • Honest pricing. Look for posted hourly rates, any registration fee and whether packages or sibling discounts are available.
  • A real day, not just a room. Ask what children actually do: play, crafts, storytime and learning activities, with limited screen time.
  • Meals and snacks. Find out whether you pack food or the center can provide it.
  • See it yourself. A good center will happily give you a tour.
