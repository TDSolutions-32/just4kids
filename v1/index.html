Questions Ask us ↗
What are your hours?

Monday–Friday 7:00 am–9:00 pm. Saturday 9:00 am–7:00 pm by reservation only. Closed Sunday.

How does drop-in care differ from full-time daycare?

Bring your child when you need it — an hour, a few hours, or the whole day — with no rigid schedule and no long-term commitment. You don't pay for time you don't use.

Can I bring my child for just a few hours?

Yes. Errands, appointments or a little time for yourself — drop off, and pick up when you're ready.

Is there a minimum age?

We welcome children 12 months and up. Camps are designed for ages 5–12.

How does enrollment work?

Drop in during operating hours and our staff will walk you through registration — or register online ahead of time. Immediate enrollment is available.

What if I have a last-minute need?

That's exactly what drop-in care is for. Last-minute appointment or emergency, you can rely on us for safe, supervised care.

Do you provide meals?

Families pack meals and snacks. It keeps costs down and makes sure every child's dietary needs and favorites are covered.

What activities do you offer?

Arts and crafts, block building, board games, music and dance, storytime, simple science experiments, indoor scavenger hunts, puzzles and sensory play.

How do you keep children safe?

All staff are CPR and first aid certified. A controlled entrance and security cameras throughout the facility, plus a smaller space for more individual attention.

How does care help prepare my child for school?

Routines, cooperation and play-based learning build early literacy, math, communication and independence — making the move to kindergarten smoother.

When is payment due?

At pickup. We accept cash, Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express.
