    How is drop-in care different from full-time daycare?

    You bring your child only when you need care, whether that's an hour, a few hours or a whole day. There's no rigid schedule or long-term commitment, and you never pay for time you don't use.

    What are your hours?

    Monday–Friday 7:00 am–9:00 pm. Saturday 9:00 am–7:00 pm by reservation only. Closed Sunday.

    Is there a minimum age?

    We welcome children 12 months and up. Camps are designed for ages 5–12.

    How does enrollment work?

    It's simple. Drop in during our hours and our friendly staff will help you register, or register online ahead of time. Enrollment is immediate and there's no long-term commitment.

    What if I have a last-minute need?

    That's exactly what drop-in care is for. Whether it's a last-minute appointment or an emergency, you can count on us for safe, supervised care.

    Do you provide meals and snacks?

    Families pack meals and snacks. That keeps costs down and makes sure every child's dietary needs and favorites are covered.

    How do you balance fun and learning?

    We build learning into play. Art, music, movement and storytelling naturally teach cognitive, social and emotional skills, so kids never feel like they're "in class."

    How does child care help prepare my child for school?

    Routines, cooperation and play-based learning build early literacy, early math, communication and independence. That makes the move to kindergarten smoother.

    When is payment due, and what do you accept?

    Payment is due at pickup. We accept cash, Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express.
