Parent notes

Why CPR-certified staff matter

Why CPR and first aid certification is one of the most important questions to ask any child care provider.

Children are curious, fast and fearless, which is exactly why emergencies can happen in a moment. Choking, falls, allergic reactions and cuts are all situations where the first few minutes matter most. Staff who are trained in CPR and first aid know what to do before help arrives.

What certification means in practice

  • Calm, fast response. Trained staff recognize an emergency and act immediately instead of freezing.
  • Correct technique. Child and infant CPR and choking response are different from adult techniques. Training covers both.
  • Everyday care. First aid training helps with the small things too, from scraped knees to knowing when a child needs a doctor.
  • Peace of mind. You can focus on your day knowing the people with your child are prepared.

Questions to ask any provider

  • Is every staff member certified, or only some?
  • How are parents contacted in an emergency?
  • What other safety measures are in place, such as a controlled entrance or cameras?

Our safety promise

At Just 4 Kids, every member of our staff is CPR and first aid certified. Our secure front door means only families and staff come inside, and security cameras throughout the facility add extra peace of mind.
