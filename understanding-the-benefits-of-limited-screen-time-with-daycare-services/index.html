Benefits of limited screen time

Why limiting screen time in child care helps young children grow, and what they do instead at Just 4 Kids.

Screens are part of modern life, and they aren't going away. But for young children, time spent watching a screen is time not spent moving, talking, building and playing with others. That's why many child care programs, including ours, keep screen time limited.

What children gain when screens are off

  • Language. Back-and-forth conversation with adults and other children builds vocabulary far better than passive watching.
  • Attention. Puzzles, building and stories help children practice focusing on one thing at a time.
  • Movement. Music, dance and active play keep growing bodies strong.
  • Social skills. Playing together teaches sharing, turn-taking and working through disagreements.
  • Creativity. Crafts, dress-up and sensory play let children invent their own ideas.

What we do instead

A day at Just 4 Kids is filled with arts and crafts, block building, dress-up play, music and dance, sensory play, storytime, puzzles and games, simple science and scavenger hunts. Play-based learning builds early literacy, early math and confidence for kindergarten.

Tips for home

  • Keep screens out of meals and the hour before bed.
  • Keep a basket of books, crayons or blocks within easy reach.
  • When screens are on, watch together and talk about what you see.
