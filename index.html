    How is drop-in care different from full-time daycare?

    You bring your child only when you need care, whether that's an hour or up to 4 hours. There's no rigid schedule or long-term commitment, and you never pay for time you don't use.

    What are your hours?

    Monday–Friday 7:00 am–9:00 pm. During the school year we open at 6:30 am so parents can drop off before work, and we take children to school. Saturday 9:00 am–7:00 pm. Closed Sunday.

    How does enrollment work?

    Drop in during our hours and we'll help you register, or register online ahead of time. Enrollment is immediate and there's no long-term commitment.

    What if I have a last-minute need?

    That's exactly what drop-in care is for. Whether it's a last-minute appointment or an emergency, you can count on us for safe, supervised care.

    Do you provide meals and snacks?

    Parents can pack their own, or we can provide meals and snacks upon request.

    How does child care help prepare my child for school?

    Routines, cooperation and play-based learning build early literacy, early math, communication and independence, so kids learn without feeling like they're "in class."

    What payment do you accept?

    Payment is due at pickup. We accept cash, Visa, MasterCard, Discover and American Express.
