Parent notes

Why local daycare matters

How a nearby, flexible child care center makes life easier for working and military families in Jacksonville.

For working parents, child care isn't just about who watches the kids. It's about the drive, the schedule and whether the plan still works when the day changes. A local center close to home and work takes a lot of stress out of that equation.

What a local center gives your family

  • Shorter commutes. Less time in the car at drop-off and pickup means more time together.
  • Flexibility. Drop-in care covers appointments, shift changes and last-minute plans without a big monthly commitment.
  • School-day support. Before- and after-school care bridges the gap between the bell and the end of the workday.
  • Community. Children see familiar faces, and parents get to know the people caring for them.

Built around Jacksonville families

Just 4 Kids is at 2038 Piney Green Rd, Unit 2, in Jacksonville, NC, and serves families from Jacksonville, Camp Lejeune, Half Moon, Richlands, Sneads Ferry, Swansboro and nearby communities. During the school year we open at 6:30am and take children to school, then offer after-school care with pickup from Hunters Creek Elementary, Morton Elementary and Carolina Forest.

We also offer sibling savings and military family pricing. Just mention it when you schedule.
